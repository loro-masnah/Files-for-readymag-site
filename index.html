Lorenzo Masnah

Colombian-born artist working at the meeting point of digital imagery and analog process. Silkscreen, stencil, plotter and laser mark-making sit alongside hand-painted gesture in collage-driven works on identity, media and cultural memory. Many of the pieces here grow out of The Noosphere Tribe, the artist's 10,000-piece generative collage collection on Ethereum, carrying its avatars, traits and raw metadata off the screen and onto linen, paper and found material.
